Canyon Vista IN Draper FAQ

What are the lease terms?

 We offer standard 6–12 month leases. 

What does it cost to move in?

$500 Security Deposit OAC and $199 Lease Initiation fee to reserve your apartment until move in.

Are utilities included in the cost?

 Utilities are billed separately from rent. Residents pay for electricity, water, sewer, and trash services. They are also responsible for the internet and cable. 

What's your pet policy?

 We are a pet-friendly community that welcomes both cats and dogs. You can have up to two pets per apartment, and breed restrictions may apply. We also have a designated pet area on-site for your convenience. Please contact our office for current rates and restrictions. 

What is your guest policy?

Tenants are responsible for their guests. Guests staying more than 14 days in a year are required to be on the lease.

What We Love About Draper

Draper, Utah, is perfectly located at the Point of the Mountain, between Salt Lake City and the peaceful beauty of the Wasatch Mountains. Whether you're exploring the city or enjoying nature, you'll have it all right at your doorstep.

Endless Outdoor Fun

Living in Draper means you're just a short drive away from top hiking trails, world-class skiing, and mountain biking. Whether you’re hitting the slopes in winter or exploring the mountains in summer, Canyon Vista gives you unbeatable access to Utah’s outdoor lifestyle.

Unique Local Attractions

Just minutes from Canyon Vista, you’ll find the Flight Park State Recreation Area, great for hang gliding and paragliding. Also nearby is South Mountain Golf Course for a round of golf, and Draper City Park with trails, playgrounds, and sports fields. There’s always something fun to do close by!

A Rich History

Draper’s history dates back to its early days as "Willow Bend." Today, it’s a mix of rich history and a growing tech scene, offering plenty of job opportunities in modern industries. With its unique blend of the past and future, Draper is the perfect place to live and work.

Tech Hub and Modern Living

As part of Silicon Slopes, Draper is quickly becoming a hotspot for tech professionals and startups. Canyon Vista is an ideal choice for those working in the growing tech industry, offering modern apartments with all the comforts you need and a great location.

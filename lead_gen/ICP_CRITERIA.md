# ICP Validation Criteria for CAD Drafting Copilot (Civil / Structural / Land Development)

## Purpose
Use this rubric to qualify firms that are likely to benefit from an AI copilot focused on **CAD drafting + iterative revisions** (2D/3D plans, markups, redlines, sheet updates, detail updates, and coordination edits).

A firm is a valid ICP target only if it demonstrates meaningful drafting/revision workflows tied to civil, structural, or land/site development deliverables.

---

## 1) Hard Inclusion Criteria (Must-Have)
A firm must satisfy **all** of the following:

1. **Discipline fit:** Primary services include at least one of:
   - Civil engineering
   - Structural engineering
   - Land development engineering
   - Site development engineering
2. **Drafting evidence:** Public evidence they produce design documents (plans, drawings, construction documents, grading/drainage/site plans, structural plans, details, sections, profiles, models, permit sets).
3. **Revision loop evidence:** Evidence of iterative updates (revisions, redlines, addenda, change orders, permit comments, plan check responses, construction administration updates).
4. **Commercial viability:** Active business presence (website + current contact channel + appears operational in the last 24 months).

If any hard inclusion criterion is missing, classify as **FAIL** regardless of score.

---

## 2) Exclusion Criteria (Auto-Disqualifiers)
Exclude immediately if any of the following are true:

- Primary focus is **not** civil/structural/land development design production.
- Firm is strictly:
  - Electrical-only engineering
  - Plumbing-only engineering
  - MEP-only with no site/structural drafting scope
  - Survey-only (no engineering design deliverables)
  - Geotechnical testing lab only (no design drafting)
  - Environmental consulting only (no CAD design production)
  - Architecture-only (unless clear structural/civil production arm exists)
- Company appears inactive, dissolved, or unreachable.
- Staffing model suggests no internal drafting/revision workflow (e.g., purely advisory/owner’s rep with no plan production).

## 5) Scoring Rubric (0–100)
Score only after passing Hard Inclusion + no Auto-Disqualifiers.

### A. Discipline & Service Fit (0–30)
- 0: Weak/unclear fit
- 10: One target discipline mentioned
- 20: Multiple target disciplines or strong specialization in one
- 30: Clear core focus in civil/structural/land development engineering

### B. Drafting Intensity (0–25)
- 0: No drafting evidence
- 10: Generic “design” language only
- 18: Explicit plan/document production
- 25: Strong drafting artifacts (plan sets, details, CDs, CAD/BIM outputs)

### C. Revision Frequency Potential (0–20)
- 0: No revision indicators
- 8: Minimal update activity implied
- 14: Regular permit/client iteration implied
- 20: Clear recurring redline/revision/change workflow

### D. Technical Environment Fit (0–15)
- 0: No tooling/workflow clues
- 5: Generic CAD mention
- 10: Specific relevant tools (AutoCAD/Civil 3D/Revit/Bluebeam)
- 15: Multiple relevant tools + drafting roles visible

### E. Commercial & Outreach Readiness (0–10)
- 0: Sparse/inactive/unreachable
- 4: Limited contactability
- 7: Active site + direct contact + identifiable decision makers
- 10: Strong market presence and reachable stakeholders

**Total Score = A + B + C + D + E (0–100)**

---

## 6) Pass/Fail Decision
- **PASS:**
  - Meets all Hard Inclusion criteria,
  - Triggers no Auto-Disqualifier,
  - **Score >= 70/100**.
- **BORDERLINE (Manual Review):** 60–69 with strong strategic reason (e.g., ideal geography, clear growth trajectory).
- **FAIL:** <60 or any Hard Inclusion miss or any Auto-Disqualifier.

Recommended operating rule for lead list quality: prioritize **75+** for outbound cold calls; reserve 60–74 for secondary nurture.

---

## 7) Data Capture Fields per Firm (for consistent validation)
For each evaluated firm, capture:
1. Firm name
2. HQ city/state (and branch near target territory, if relevant)
3. Primary discipline(s)
4. Evidence URLs (services page, projects page, careers page)
5. Drafting evidence found (keywords/artifacts)
6. Revision-loop evidence found
7. Exclusion check result
8. Score by section (A/B/C/D/E)
9. Total score
10. Final decision (PASS / BORDERLINE / FAIL)
11. Notes for call personalization (project types, software clues, hiring for CAD roles)

This ensures repeatable qualification and easier deduplication/auditing.
